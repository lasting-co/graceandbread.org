Grace and Bread exists to show grace and share bread—serving our neighbors in practical ways and pointing people toward the love of Jesus.

Grace and Bread began out of a risky and spontaneous adventure by a few people wanting to help their neighbors on the street, with the intention of showing grace and offering the bread of life.
